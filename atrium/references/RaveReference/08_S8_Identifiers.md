> Identifiers
>
> Identifiers are the names of rules, tables, etc. An identifier is a (non-empty) sequence of letters (A-Z and a-z only), digits and underscores (\_). Case is not significant, a\_name and A\_Name are considered equal. An identifier must not begin with a digit or underscore and it must not have the same spelling as a keyword or a reserved word.
