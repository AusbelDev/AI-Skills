> Conditional expressions
>
> Syntax
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | expr | ::= | if expr then expr else expr |
>
> The condition must be boolean. If it istrue the expression after then is returned, otherwise the expression after else is returned. The expressions after then and else must be of the same type and this also applies to the complete if-then-else expression.
