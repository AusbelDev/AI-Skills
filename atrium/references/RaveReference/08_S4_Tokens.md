> Tokens
>
> The Rave compiler reads the rule set and collects the individual characters into a sequence of lexical tokens, which are then processed further by the compiler. There are seven token classes: operators, separators, variables, identifiers, keywords, reserved words and constants.
>
> When forming tokens, the compiler always produces the longest token possible. Notation is therefore interpreted as the single wordnotation rather than the unary operator not followed by ation. The latter interpretation is achieved by inserting white space between the two parts.
