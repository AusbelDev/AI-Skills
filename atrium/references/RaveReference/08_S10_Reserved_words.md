> Reserved words
>
> The reserved words are a number of identifiers that have special meaning to Rave. Among them are all the built-in functions and traversers:
>
> abs, abstime, accumulator, add\_months, add\_weeks, add\_years, alias, all, and, and2, any, avg, backwards, bool, booster\_assertion, build, by, cache, capi\_legs, capi\_time, cat, concat, constraint, context, count, cost, current, current\_context, deadline, default, delta, durable, earliest, else, end, enddate, endday, enum, export, external, failtext, failobject, false, filter, first, format\_int, foreach, format\_time, forwards, from, global, group, if, import, in, index, inherits, int, interval\_end, interval\_start, is\_first, is\_last, iterator, last, latest, length, let, level, locate, map, matrix, max, max\_penalty, maxvalue, min, min\_penalty, minvalue, mod, module, msgr, msgx, n, next, nmax, nmin, not, off, on, or, or2, overlap, overshoot\_max\_limit, overshoot\_min\_limit, parameter, partition, prev, redefine, reltime, remark, require, root, round\_down, round\_down\_month, round\_down\_offset, round\_down\_week, round\_down\_year, round\_up, round\_up\_month, round\_up\_offset, round\_up\_week, round\_up\_year, rule, rule\_on, scale\_time, set, severity, sqrt, startdate, startday, string, string\_to\_int, substr, sum, table, then, time, times\_index, time\_of\_day, time\_of\_week, to, total\_rule\_penalty, transform, true, tw\_while, use, valid, void, when, where, while, y
>
> Contexts, transforms and built-in iterators are also reserved words. Contexts and transforms are system-configurable, similar to the keywords.
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Note | The wordcost is treated in a special way by the Rave compiler. It can be used as a module name, but not as a general identifier. |
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Note | It is strongly recommended not to use reserved words in Rave Publisher PDL in Rave code. |
