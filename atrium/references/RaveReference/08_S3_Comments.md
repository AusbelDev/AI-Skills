> Comments
>
> A comment in the language begins with the characters/\* and ends with the first subsequent occurrence of the characters \*/. Comments may contain any number of characters and are treated as white space by the compiler.
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Example | The following example shows a legal comment:  /\* A comment \*/ |
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Note | Comments cannot be nested. This example is not a legal construction:/\* The following part has been commented out:  %aa% = 13; /\* The variable a \*/  %bb% = 17; /\* The variable b \*/ \*/ |
