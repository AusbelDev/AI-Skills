> Precedence and associativity of operators
>
> When an expression contains more than one operator and there are no parentheses to indicate how to group the operands, Rave uses the following rules to determine the order of evaluation. Each operator has a precedence.
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Operator | Precedence |
> | \* / mod | Highest |
> | + - |  |
> | = <> < <= > >= in not |  |
> | and |  |
> | or | Lowest |
>
> An operator with a higher precedence is always performed before one of lower precedence (higher precedence binds harder). When an expression contains more than one operator of the same precedence, the expression is evaluated from left to right.
