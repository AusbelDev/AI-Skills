> Variables
>
> Variables are the names assigned to value and function definitions. A variable can be any name surrounded by percent signs. Variable names are case-insensitive. Non-alphanumeric characters may be used in variables, but are not distinguished from one another.
>
> |  |  |
> | --- | --- |
> | Example | %VAR1\_Begin% and %var1-beGIN% are the same  %var1begin% and %var1\_begin% are different variables |
>
> As a matter of style variable names should consist of only letters (A-Z and a-z), digits and underscores (\_). Avoid national characters.
